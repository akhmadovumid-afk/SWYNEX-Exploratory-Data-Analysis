SWYNEX Internship — Task 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

📌 Project Overview

This repository contains the Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) performed on the cleaned e-commerce sales dataset (cleaned_ecommerce_sales.xlsx). The objective of this task is to analyze sales distributions, regional performance, product performance, and customer metrics to derive actionable business insights.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

Microsoft Excel: Data aggregation, PivotTables, Descriptive Statistics, PivotCharts.

Markdown: Documentation for GitHub Portfolio.

📊 Key Findings & Visualizations

1. Revenue by Product Category

Electronics is the leading revenue driver ($1.83M), followed closely by Clothing ($1.53M).

Beauty ($765K) and Home ($982K) show lower revenue contributions, highlighting an opportunity for targeted promotional campaigns.

2. Regional Sales Performance

West region generated the highest overall revenue (~$1.35M).

North closely follows as the second strongest region, while East and South remain steady, indicating balanced regional demand.

💡 Top 5 Business Insights & Recommendations

Focus Marketing on Top Performers: Electronics and Clothing account for over 65% of total revenue. Reallocate marketing budgets to capitalize on these high-converting product categories.

Targeted Regional Growth in the West: The West region exhibits the highest purchasing power. Optimize fulfillment centers in the West to maintain fast delivery speeds.

Cross-Selling Strategies for Low-Performing Categories: Implement product bundling (e.g., combining Home items with Electronics) to boost sales in lower-revenue categories like Home and Beauty.

Discount Optimization: Evaluate discount structures across product lines to ensure profit margins are preserved while driving order volumes.

Logistics Efficiency: Maintain delivery lead times across all regions to protect customer satisfaction and positive product ratings.
